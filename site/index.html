Unmapped chapter codes — flagged, not hidden

194.25 miles carry a chapter code that maps to no chapter, or no chapter at all. They are in the trail total and in the state table; only their chapter attribution is missing. About 131 miles carry the literal code “None” (mostly North Dakota); ADK-ON is probably an Adirondack data-entry variant (Finger Lakes Trail segments); GTR sits in a proposed-reroute area; LON is the New Rockford Canal stretch. Star of the North (STN) shows only 10.21 tagged miles though the chapter maintains about 27 — its remaining miles are probably tagged under other codes or “None”.
